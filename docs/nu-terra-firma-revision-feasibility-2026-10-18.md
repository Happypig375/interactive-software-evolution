## Observation and falsification

The source-derived prediction is that whole-property propagation can preserve
the local target's roughness by also retaining its old albedo. That would be
adverse delivery, even when predicted. Batch field updates are predicted to
deliver both requirements. Any coherent contrary observation revises the
prediction. Collateral configuration changes also count as adverse.

Loaded, Ready, Prepared, Applied and Settled captures have fixed callback
relationships. Setup is checked before the operation; desired final values
never choose the capture phase, retry or stopping rule. Reopens capture two
consecutive phases without an edit. Every original explicit property and the
declared effective property set are observed on all 197 originals. Only the
six named transform fields receive finite absolute tolerance `0.0001`.

The public lifecycle observer counts callbacks before classifying them,
records all callback kinds and addresses, and retains an independent final
counter after cleanup. Contiguous ordinals test recorder integrity; event
transitions are reconciled with captured address/ID maps. Endpoint IDs alone
cannot detect a lost balanced intermediate creation/removal pair. These checks
still cannot prove that every internal or native action emitted a delivered
callback.

Source and graph predict 31 direct palm reconstructions plus three 44-entity
Villa trees: 163 original-entity occurrences over 139 distinct addresses,
including 24 palms twice. Batch predicts zero. Those numbers are falsifiable
predictions, never validity filters. Coherent differing counts remain evidence.
Recorded phase/process times include instrumentation, whose work differs with
event volume; no speed or complete-work estimate is assigned.
