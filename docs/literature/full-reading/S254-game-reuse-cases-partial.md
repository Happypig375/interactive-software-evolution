## Cases, method and evidence units

Sections3–4 describe two small studios, four principal developer participants and five games. Kraken Empire contributes Kromaia and Toy Tactics; Entalto contributes NeonHAT, Grabitoons! and VTM: Heartless Lullaby. Four games use Unity; Kromaia uses a proprietary C++ engine with XML-defined reusable objects. These purposively selected cases span genres and project sizes; they are not a population prevalence estimate or matched engine/language comparison.

The sequence combines two initial questionnaires/interviews, seven game-specific responses, two studio focus groups and one Entalto visit. Three researchers conduct/debrief the discussions, and participants review the resulting account. Five project trees support interpretation of component relationships. Their edges represent inferred similarity relationships, **not necessarily chronological ancestry**; the conclusion explicitly leaves temporal study to future work. Repeated evidence about these projects does not create independent games or participants.

Preserve the positive findings: participants describe centralized configuration, reuse of object behavior/assets, and a level editor that lets designers produce levels with fewer reported mistakes. Setup work, propagated errors and ad hoc delivery pressure coexist with those benefits. Developers recognize similar score objects, dancers and walls, and identify unused effects or abandoned mechanics. These are useful situated observations; the study does not compare completed repairs with and without trees. Different developers also interpret cluster shape differently, citing both reuse management and game-content variation.

## Consequence for Nu and continuation

For B03/B04/B10/B12, include configured objects, assets and dependencies in the change boundary. Useful existing engines already support object reuse; similarity visualization can expose relationships that folder organization leaves unclear. Nu's proposed value must be tied to a concrete improvement in preserving or changing those obligations, with learning/setup and asset integration included. A compiler check on source cases is not evidence that all configured-object behavior was preserved.
