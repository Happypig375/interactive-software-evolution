## S290: adaptation can serve a purpose

The2021 study interviews one senior leader from each of eight studios employing at least five full-time staff. Sessions typically last40–60minutes. Nine initial questions precede framework-practice lists. Selective transcripts are offered for correction; General Inductive Approach analysis includes another researcher's parallel coding and two stakeholder checks. The interview was not piloted; the collection window and overlap with2019 participants are unreported.

Participants describe useful combinations of iterative feature work, continuous asset production, milestones and changing development phases. Some favor Scrumban or collocation; training, leadership and shared-understanding problems persist. These are reported experiences, without observed workflows or comparative cost measurements.

TableI and its discussion associate particular practices with named difficulties. Absence of the label “communication” does not establish absence of overlapping problems such as knowledge silos. Eight interviews cannot identify the claimed causal explanation. Preserve useful adaptations alongside the reported difficulties.
