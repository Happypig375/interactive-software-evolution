## What the conference method establishes

**Positive mechanism and scoped outcomes (S280 §§III–V, indexed pp.3–9).** Five open-source games span JavaScript, Java, C and C++: Pacman Canvas, Infinite Mario, Pingus, Chocolate Doom and Bos Wars. Manual instrumentation exports selected state through XML templates to a separate BeepBeep process. Temporal formulas relate successive snapshots; testers still play the games. Four games use deliberately seeded faults; Bos Wars uses three historical gameplay reports selected from36 tracker entries. Successful detection is reported. The final Bos Wars instrumentation/property task reportedly took under an hour after experience with the other games.

**Measured versus inferred cost (§V).** For Pingus on the stated Ubuntu12.04.2/2GHz dual-core/4GB machine, the paper reports167 events/s, about700 bytes/event, approximately0.4ms instrumentation CPU time/event, 0.13ms for P1 and an early0.3ms/event region for P5. The6.7% slowdown,39.4fps example and capacity for over50 properties are estimates from these measurements. No matched uninstrumented replay, comparative tester-time study, defect-recall denominator or net maintenance-cost estimate is supplied. One representative trace is plotted; trace similarity is asserted without a reported distribution. Automatic bug filing remains future work in the conclusion.

## Reconstruction and implications for Nu

The observation contract has three separate parts: which state the game exports, which history the monitor remembers, and which condition counts as failure. Centralizing the hook reduces insertion sites, but it does not eliminate data extraction or specification work. A stable snapshot schema can insulate the monitor from implementation changes only while the exported values retain their intended meaning. A change inside one update that is undone before observation may be invisible. These are consequences of the reconstructed interface, not measured missed-bug rates.

P3 correlates the same character’s position across adjacent snapshots through nested quantification. Under the printed universal-quantifier semantics, disappearance from the next snapshot need not fail this formula; any required persistence/exit condition is a separate obligation. P4’s informal requirement concerns eventual cessation of bashing, but its actual formula is omitted. The text does not settle how end-of-level or an unfinished obligation is scored. P5’s cheaper empty-domain phase also shows why average monitor cost depends on the trace’s active entities and obligations.

The paper’s independent process and one-way channel distinguish monitoring from [S277/S278’s host-applied repair](S277-S278-mayet-runtime-repair.md). A monitor’s verdict does not itself change the game. Process separation also does not establish universal freedom from serialization, queueing or shared-resource cost. The favorable timings establish feasibility under the reported conditions; they do not prove every game, formula or later architecture has the same overhead.

## Disposition and consequential continuation

Preserve the positive multi-game instrumentation and selected-fault detection evidence. Its contribution is an observation architecture plus demonstrated checking at reported costs. It does not isolate a language effect, show semantic preservation after arbitrary edits, or measure the whole cost of maintaining specifications and instrumentation. For Nu, the useful comparison is exported-state fidelity, temporal history/termination, actual defect outcomes and integration effort; diagnostic visibility alone remains insufficient.
