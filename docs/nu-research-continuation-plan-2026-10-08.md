## Objective and evidence boundary

Determine which inspected Nu mechanisms help preserve correct behavior, what they cost, and how they affect controlled agent maintenance. Human-steered development is the intended practical context. Its anticipated importance in 2027 is a planning assumption supplied by the user, not an observed prevalence or economic result.

The near-term evidence has three distinct levels: a source-supported capability/contract; its measured correctness and resource behavior under specified conditions; and outcomes of bounded agent changes. None alone establishes human productivity or the economics of steering. Human-only and agent-only workflows remain useful bounded cases; they are not equally weighted primary populations or mandatory arms of every study.

The user raised convergence in agent-generated hypotheses and solutions as a research risk, alongside the effort of human-only work. Address this through task provenance, contrasting requirements, alternatives and honest transfer limits. Do not claim that an instruction to be diverse resolves it or that repeated model outputs are independent scientific corroboration.

## Rules for bounded agent evidence

Derive requirements from pinned source contracts, documented practice, literature and verified development history where available. The current source-inspired examples are not claimed to be mined Nu issues. Record each family's provenance before candidate outcomes, including its strongest rival and a situation where the other approach could work better.

Include local and coordinated changes; useful and redundant diagnostics; valid existing defaults; known and unknown faults; and recoverable and irreversible effects when relevant. These are dimensions of meaningful coverage, not a factorial mandate or paper/task quota. Cosmetic variants of one requirement remain one family.

Specify equal intended requirements, initial-state contracts, agent configuration, available tools/information and resource rules within each comparison. Preserve independent workspaces and all assigned attempts. Correct reorganization is allowed. No preference for the initial source style is part of behavioral success.

Keep actual human input, AI synthesis and any independent review distinct. Research-design critique does not automatically make the critic an experimental participant, but neither may unrecorded human solutions enter an autonomous candidate condition. A rescued result never replaces the original failure. Historical E/H outcomes and adverse compactness evidence remain intact.

Report verified behavior alongside total resource use across all attempts. Build success, diagnostic counts, patch spread, source size and recurring solution patterns are explanatory observations. Do not condition the main effect on successful compilation, receipt of a diagnostic, conformity to style or eventual human intervention.

Task families/applications bound transfer; repeated executions estimate executor variability. Broader model or application claims require additional evidence. The designs specify their contrasts now and assign precision, meaningful-effect and allocation decisions to explicit feasibility dependencies rather than inventing sample sizes or borrowing old run allowances.
