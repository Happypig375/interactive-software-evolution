Score these channels separately; a single process-success flag is insufficient:

1. **Provenance/completeness:** assignment, current build/source/PDB/host,
   original/staged hashes, predecessor receipt and pair, protected inputs,
   actual loaded assemblies/runtime, bounded execution, captures and cleanup.
2. **Actual input and helper semantics:** independent live 32-member maps for
   load/prepare, exact operation roster and requested draft derived from the

3. **Native delivery:** all 16 material fields at every phase; managed values

4. **Cumulative requirements:** the nominal authored values/modes across the
   five stages, including failures that a later stage happens to repair.
5. **Persistence:** saved descriptor versus Settled, AfterSave nonmutation,
   exact-byte companion binding, next fresh load versus actual saved descriptor
   and predecessor endpoint. Report saved divergence and reopen divergence
   separately, even if either happens to satisfy nominal intent.
6. **Collateral state:** all 197 original logical addresses, 19 roots, names,
   parents, dispatchers, persistence/protection, original child membership,
   all properties except the two permitted material fields and fresh current
   Order numbers. Use the old six-transform tolerance only for transforms;
   otherwise use structural equality and retain missing/None/Some distinctions.
7. **Links/history:** all 166 original source links must preserve and resolve;

8. **Order/identity/lifecycle:** record raw GetOrder and child address for each

An observed duplicate order or collateral violation is an adverse outcome,
not permission to discard the attempt. Original-stage baseline checks apply
only to edit-1; later stages must not require all-None materials or a nominally
successful predecessor. An actual missing target or nonfinite material can
make the workflow unable to proceed; report that as observed inability when
provenance is intact, distinct from faulty instrumentation.

evidence and may advance as a complete pair. A recorder defect, malformed or
missing required capture, missing lifecycle ordinal, unreconciled counter or
failed cleanup makes required observation coverage incomplete and prevents
advancement. Label the affected channel invalid, not a Nu behavior failure.

channels rather than treating an expected roster count as provenance by itself.

retained and paired. A complete adverse pair remains the next assigned input.
Pair completeness requires bounded parseable scene bytes and independently
valid metadata binding, not successful nominal values, helper delivery or a
second component load with a good material map. In particular, an actual bad
material map may make the next load reject; that is a workflow outcome, not
proof that the preceding byte pair was unbound.

Thrown setter/observer/save/bind/write, timeout, over-limit output, missing

advance. Separate incomplete workflow failure from invalid observation or
